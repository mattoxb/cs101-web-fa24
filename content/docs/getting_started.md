## Welcome to CS 101!

### Quick Start

- Sign up for our [CampusWire forum]({{<param "campuswire">}}).  You will be responsible for announcements posted there, so be sure to check it at least once a day.  Urgent announcements
  will be posted there and emailed to the class.  The join code is {{<param "campuswireJoin">}}. 
- Register for the course on [PrairieLearn]({{<param "prairielearn" >}}).  This is where almost all assignments will happen.
- Prairielearn will show you the scores for your individual assignments, but if you want a summary report and resulting letter grade report, follow the instructions on the [your grades page]({{< relref "github_repository">}}).
- You should now review the [Syllabus]({{<relref "syllabus">}}).

### Quick Facts

- Lectures are Monday and Wednesday at 11:00 in the Lincoln Hall Theater, [located here](https://w3w.co/appear.remain.groups).

  - The [First lecture]({{<relref "../lectures/course_intro">}}) is  {{<lectureDate "course_intro">}}
  - See the [Schedule]({{<relref "schedule">}}) for a complete list.
  
- Labs happen throughout the week.
  - You can look up your lab time, location, and TA at the [TA Labs and Office Hours]({{<relref "tas">}}).  This will be populated by the end of the first week of class.
  - New this semester: the labs will be in the CIF.
  - Attendance is required!
